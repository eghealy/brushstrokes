Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28 · ← Back to Brushstrokes

The short version

Brushstrokes doesn't have a server, a database, or any way to receive your writing even if it wanted to. When you connect a Google Doc, your browser talks directly to Google using your own sign-in. Everything is computed on your device and disappears when you close the tab. No AI ever sees your document, and nothing is ever stored, logged, or sold.

What data this site accesses

When you click "Connect Google Doc," Google asks you to sign in and pick a document using Google's own file picker. That grants Brushstrokes the drive.file permission scope — access to read only the specific document you selected, nothing else in your Drive. Brushstrokes uses that access to read the document's text and revision history directly from Google's API, in your browser.

What happens to that data

All processing — comparing revisions, computing which words and sentences were edited, rendering the heatmap and the downloadable graphic — happens locally in your browser's memory using plain, deterministic code (no AI, no machine learning). Nothing is sent to a Brushstrokes server, because there isn't one. Nothing is sent to any AI model or service. Nothing is written to a database, a log file, or any form of persistent storage. When you close the tab or navigate away, everything is gone.

Cookies and local storage

Brushstrokes does not use cookies, local storage, or any other browser storage mechanism to track you or remember anything between visits.

Third-party services this page loads

Loading this page requests a few resources from third-party services, each governed by that provider's own privacy policy:

• Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — for the page's typefaces.
• Google Identity Services and Google Picker (accounts.google.com, apis.google.com) — power the sign-in and file-selection flow described above.
• Google Drive API (googleapis.com) — reads your selected document's text and revision history, called directly from your browser.
• jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) — hosts a small open-source text-comparison library used to compute the heatmap.

None of these services receive your document content from Brushstrokes beyond the Google API calls described above, which go directly from your browser to Google.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and Brushstrokes does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
